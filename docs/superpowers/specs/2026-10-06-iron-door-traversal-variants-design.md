Approved in conversation: one Iron Door card, Enter/Leave switches complete
presets and the URL. Preserve `biohazard-1996-a01-iron-door` as Enter; add
`biohazard-1996-a01-iron-door-leave`. Variant names are application conventions,
not claims about the original room interior/exterior.

Add optional traversal and variantOf registry metadata. Leave is a complete
right-hinged preset using the Enter back texture as its viewed front and Enter
front as its back. Assets remain shared. Its independently fitted timeline uses
the source's second transition around 10.65–15.35 s: slow approach, subtle early
opening, accelerating swing until the face narrows, then fade. Renderer direction
is a fitting parameter, while UI calls these Enter/Leave.

Group registered variants for catalog cards and the detail preset picker. Resolve
all IDs for direct links, and show Enter/Leave on detail for grouped presets.

updates URL, markers, properties, usage code, and full-screen request. Keep the
existing direction inspector for ungrouped single doors. Random mode continues
choosing from complete registered presets.
